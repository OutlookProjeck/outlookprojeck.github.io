Privacy policy — Outlook Publisher

Outlook Publisher is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Outlook Documentary and Second Bill, for those channels.

With the owner's permission it uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload and schedule the owner's videos, manage playlists and channel settings, read the owner's channel statistics, and read and reply to comments on the owner's videos.

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Comment text is processed only to prepare replies for the owner to approve.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
